# Portfolio title

**Maker.** Your name (or a pseudonym if you prefer).
**Tools.** Name, model, version or date, for each.

## The four assignments
A1, A2, the ethics essay, and A3, each revised after feedback. Under each, two or three sentences on what the feedback said, what you changed, and why.

## The final piece
Embed the media so that a reader meets the work first. A live piece becomes a recording, an interactive piece is embedded or linked, and a printed or 3D object is documented with photographs or a short video. Name the data types, at least two, for example text and image.

## The brief
Who the final piece is for and what it had to do.

## Contributions
If the final piece was made in a group, name the other members and say what each person contributed, including you. Leave this out if you worked alone.

## Reflection
1 500 to 2 000 words, as text in the exposition, covering the whole portfolio. Cover the briefs and the audiences, the tools with versions, and a timeline of decisions from the first assignment to the final piece. Answer the two questions, where the AI surprised you and where you exerted your will, pointing at a specific moment in a specific artefact. Then take one ethical question you ran into and how you resolved it, and say what you would do differently.

## Provenance
Human edits. Any voices or likenesses used and the consent obtained.

## Prompt log
The full log, linked or attached, grown from your weekly practice log.

## Before you hand in
- [ ] Every video, sound, and embedded piece plays in a private browser window through the URL.
- [ ] The exposition is shared with the examiners, not published.
- [ ] The URL, a PDF copy of the exposition, the reflection, and the prompt log are handed in in Canvas.

## After the grade
- [ ] Keep it private.
- [ ] Delete it.
- [ ] Self-publish it, with a Creative Commons licence (CC BY 4.0 by default). Publishing is permanent and cannot be undone, any consent you obtained must cover it, and group members must agree before a shared piece is published.
